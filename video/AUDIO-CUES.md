## Mix

`tools/mix.mjs`: the bed ducks under each line (starting 0.25 s before it, releasing over 0.6 s after it, a
pause of 1 s or more inside a line lets it rise) and crossfades there to a copy with the voice's band carved
out, line by line 20 dB under the voice; the effects sit about 24 dB under the voice while it speaks and 16 dB
under it in a gap; the master is -14 LUFS integrated with the true peak at or under -1.5 dBTP, measured on
the exported MP4. The product's own sound in the captures stays muted (`FILM.capture`): the film adds its own cues.
