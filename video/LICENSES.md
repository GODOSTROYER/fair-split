The eight CC0 files are copied byte for byte from the Barty-Bart motion-graphics sound-effects kit
(https://github.com/Barty-Bart/motion-graphics, `skills/sound-effects/sfx`, MIT for its code; its trimmed and
normalised previews of the sounds above). Each sound's own page read "Creative Commons 0" on 5 Nov 2026. No Mixkit
file is used or copied: Mixkit's licence bars redistribution. The sound-pass method (a cue list from the animation,
one real sound per cue) is the kit's, credited there to Vox (@Voxyz_ai).

The music is CC BY 4.0: it is cut and faded for the film (`tools/build-music-bed.mjs`), never re-orchestrated,
and its credit must appear in the YouTube video's description (the artist's Content ID matches on it). `CREDITS.txt`
holds the exact lines, ready for the YouTube and Devpost descriptions; the end card carries a short credit
(`FILM.musicCredit`). The tracks' WAVs are Patreon-only, so the free MP3s (320 kb/s) are the sources.

Mona/FS Sans supplies every word and figure on screen. The OFL texts are bundled with line endings and trailing
spaces normalized. No third-party artwork, logos or photographs are included. HyperFrames 0.8.157 is the external,
pinned Apache-2.0 build/render tool; it is not vendored into this project. F3's captures still need their own
provenance and review before final delivery.
