# Fair Split film · script v4 (ADR-0017)

Approved builder brief: assemble the F1 v4 film, 175 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps.
A product video: a judge understands everything in the first minute, then sees it
work in depth, then reads the limits. Original cards and an animated architecture,
truthful capture placeholders, captions from the script, no music, local repo fonts.
F3 captures and F4 Gemini voice come later. Do not change spoken lines or timings.

Viewer arc: the problem (handwritten weigh-slips, a duplicate pink copy, a fine no
rule allows, members who cannot check, a buyer who cannot prove); how Fair Split
solves it in one breath; the architecture, drawn as it is named; a fast reel of what
is special on the site (the split matrix, the mass-balance dial, the rate card that
refuses, two keys, Proof, the member's audio and peso trace, Try to break it). Then
the deep dive on the real product: the slips and the pink carbon, funding, reading,
count it once, holds in ink, two keys, payouts and the move to cash, Proof, the
member's peso and sentence, and why it holds up. Then limits, impact, the end card.
Motion is evidence: card lines and architecture steps appear on their words; product
transitions belong only to the captures. No ornamental movement, inferred payment
status or timers.
