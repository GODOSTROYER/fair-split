## What we are building

### The product on one page

Bulawanon Kakaw Growers MPC (Calinan, Davao City; 64 members) ships lot LB-PH-2026-03 (9,625 kg of cacao, 9,240 kg Grade 1) to Lantern Bay Chocolate Co.; the lot price is wired outside PayPal. For Season 2026-A Lantern Bay pays one PayPal invoice of US$4,333.50: premium 2,887.50, quality bonus 1,386.00, disbursement costs 60.00. All of it is synthetic, generated by `packages/seed`.
1. **Ratify.** Claude reads Board Resolution BR 2026-04 into rules with verbatim quotes; Secretary and Chair ratify it on 14 Mar; its hash is bolted before slip 0301.
2. **Invoice.** Code computes the quote; Claude drafts the invoice through Agent Toolkit 1.11.0 behind a policy lattice; the treasurer approves the send; AUTO_CANCEL is set.
3. **Fund.** A fresh GET showing PAID or MARKED_AS_PAID with 0.00 due makes the run FUNDED; the verified INVOICING.INVOICE.PAID corroborates.
4. **Read.** Claude reads each slip twice; code scores agreement; doubt goes to a person.
5. **Compute and hold.** A deterministic engine computes 64 lines in integer cents and holds what it cannot justify: H1, slip 0457 counted twice (white original and pink copy); H2, Merlyn's 2.14 kg per tree against 1.6; H3, a ₱200 fine no rule allows; slip 0462's stained digit, entered by hand; the mass-balance dial.
6. **Approve.** Analyn resolves holds and holds to sign; Ernesto co-signs on his phone because the batch exceeds US$300.
7. **Pay.** The typed Payouts tool re-hashes and posts 8 items (US$368.39); verified item webhooks flip statements; Rhea's item parks UNCLAIMED and moves to cash.
8. **Explain and prove.** Each member reads or hears (Cebuano, Filipino, English) why her number is her number, down to her slip; Lantern Bay re-checks the run in its own browser.

End state: 7 members paid by PayPal (US$341.74), 57 in cash (US$3,876.43), US$21.14 kept for members, 64 of 64 lines explained.

**Line.** "Claude writes in pencil. People sign in ink. PayPal makes it carbon." Sub-line: "Every peso, traced to the pencil mark." Invariant: the model reads paper; code computes; people approve; PayPal executes.

**Who pays, and why PayPal.** Lantern Bay: Fairtrade's cocoa standard already requires per-member premium receipts in Côte d'Ivoire and Ghana, and EU rules on unproven claims apply from 27 Sep 2026, so buyers want line-level evidence. The co-op is merchant of record; Fair Split never holds funds. Philippine PayPal accounts withdraw locally and to GCash. In production the brand pays a per-run audit fee (OD-10).

## Experience and design direction

### Art direction

A pay run made physical. Paper is the only thing that casts a shadow: Jessie's slips on a desk the colour of a tablea disc. Machine output prints flat in graphite with a provenance footer and never gets a button (**pencil**). A person's decision is a square slab whose depth matches its consequence (3 px reversible, 5 px changes a line, 8 px plus a hold moves money or signs), stamped with initials (**ink**). Ratified rules are bolted and refuse even the treasurer. An approved amount stays hollow until PayPal confirms it, then turns jade flush on every copy at once (**carbon**).
- **Themes:** Tablea (cacao-lacquer dark) for the console; Adlaw (daylight) for app and Proof; one theme per surface.
- **One job per colour:** jade is money (raised: will move it; flush: PayPal confirmed); canary is a person's decision, never an error; red only a PayPal failure; hatch is cash or unavailable, with a word; pod orange only focus and the dial needle. No purple, gradients, glass, glow or PayPal blues.
- **Type:** Mona Sans for UI and numbers (tabular, slashed zero), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for statements, Big Shoulders Stencil for lot marks; no monospace.
- **Motion is evidence:** machines print (tweens), people press (springs, depth), PayPal lands. State animates only on an SSE event, a 201/409 or a signature; no timers, count-ups or confetti; reduced motion keeps every phase.
- **Sound and haptics** decorate, never inform: tick, plunk, clack, chime, one pentatonic note per verified payout; web sound starts off.

### State machines (pure tables; compare-and-set on state and version)

- **Run:** DRAFT → QUOTED → INVOICED → FUNDED → EXTRACTING → COMPUTED → IN_REVIEW → TREASURER_APPROVED → (AWAITING_BOARD) → APPROVED → POSTING → POSTED → SETTLING → (NEEDS_FOLLOW_UP) → SETTLED → CLOSED; exits CANCELLED, DENIED, HALTED. A line edit after key 1 voids both approvals; unknown invoice statuses change nothing and alert `/ops`.
- **Line:** COMPUTED → READY or HELD (→ READY or EXCLUDED); READY → OFF_RAIL_DUE → OFF_RAIL_PAID; READY → LOCKED → SUBMITTED (before the POST) → PAYPAL_PENDING → PAID, UNCLAIMED, PAYPAL_HELD, NEEDS_ACTION or REVERSED; UNCLAIMED → RETURNED → OFF_RAIL_DUE (re-route is P1).
- **Items** only move to a higher rank (PENDING 10 … SUCCESS 60, REVERSED 70); RETURNED only after our own cancel.
- **Holds:** duplicate, yield, low confidence on kilos, serial, member or grade ("By hand") and mass balance block approval; no-rule, rate-mismatch and injection holds reserve the line, never redistributed. Skipping 0462 blocks: "Skipped. Approval waits for slip 0462." (C-17). Slip 0449's injected note is a quarantine notice, not a hold.
